# Phase 6 — Ecosystem

*24+ months · Market leadership stage*

Long-horizon, sequenced by dependency rather than fixed dates.

- **QRToken Customer Wallet** — unified loyalty points redeemable across every merchant on the platform
- **White-label solution** for hotel chains and corporate cafeterias, themeable per brand
- **Data marketplace** — anonymized, city-level F&B trend reports for FMCG and ingredient suppliers
- **Franchise management** — a multi-level dashboard layer between Super Admin and individual Owner dashboards
- **Accounting integrations** — direct sync with Tally, Zoho Books, Khatabook
- **Voice ordering** for accessibility on the customer site

## Exit criteria

This phase is vision-stage and sequenced by dependency rather than a fixed exit gate; each initiative above ships and is evaluated independently as the platform approaches the 2-million-merchant target.
